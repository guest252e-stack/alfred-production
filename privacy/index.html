Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 (TikTok section added)

Who uses this application

Alfred_production is a private tool. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Alfred Production, alfred_production_en and Super Alfred, on their own channels. It has no other users and offers no sign-up.

What Google data it accesses

Through the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) and the OAuth scope youtube.force-ssl, granted by the channel owner, the application reads and manages that owner's own channel: the list of their uploaded videos and their status; the title, description, tags, language, privacy status and scheduled release time of those videos; thumbnails and caption files; comments the channel posts under its own videos. It does not access other people's channels, contacts, e-mail or any other Google data.

TikTok

The same owner also uses the tool, through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, to post their own short videos to their own TikTok account. With the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish granted by that account, the tool reads the account's basic profile (display name, avatar, posting options) and uploads videos with their captions and privacy settings chosen by the owner. It reads no other TikTok data (no followers, messages, likes or other users' content). TikTok access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions).

How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's own videos as described above, on YouTube and TikTok. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is never sold, rented or shared with third parties. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's private server, in files readable only by the owner's account. Video information is kept in local working files on that same server. Nothing is stored by any third party.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google account permissions page; the local credential files are then deleted. YouTube data held by Google can be managed through YouTube and Google account settings.

YouTube, Google and TikTok policies

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok features are subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.
