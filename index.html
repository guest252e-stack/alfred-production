Alfred_production

Alfred_production is a private publishing tool used by one creator to manage their own YouTube channels: Alfred Production (educational videos in French), alfred_production_en (the same kind of videos in English) and Super Alfred.

The creator uploads the videos to YouTube Studio by hand. The tool then uses the YouTube Data API, with the creator's own authorization, to:

  • set the title, description, tags and language of those videos;
  • schedule their public release date;
  • set their thumbnails and caption files;
  • post a comment from the channel under its own Shorts, linking to the full video.

It also posts the same creator's short videos to their own TikTok account (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API), with the caption, privacy and interaction settings the creator chooses.

It is not a public service: it acts only on the channels of the person who authorized it, and has no other users.

Français : outil privé de publication qui gère uniquement les chaînes YouTube de son créateur (titres, descriptions, dates de sortie, vignettes, commentaires). Voir la politique de confidentialité.
